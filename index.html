How traits and descriptions affect gameplay

Friendly or unfriendly wording in a description affects starting relationships. Traits are generated randomly for creatures. Creature trait modifier sizes are randomized and shown in each character's trait stats.

    Weapons and tools must be crafted as a turn action using gathered resources. Building a shop or house also requires a hammer.

      Create Your Character

      Choose up to three traits. A counter trait will be assigned randomly when you start. Positive traits add 2 to their stat checks; the counter trait subtracts 2. Strong/Weak and Resilient/Fragile also change max HP by 5.

      What each trait does

      Descriptions affect starting relationships only. For your character, positive traits add 2 and the counter trait subtracts 2 from d20 checks using that stat. Traits that list HP also change your max HP.
